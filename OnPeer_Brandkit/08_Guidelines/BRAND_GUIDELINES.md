# OnPeer brand guidelines | Version 2.0

## Identity
Approved direction: OnPeer 02 from the nine-concept infinity-P sheet. The mark pairs a flowing exchange loop with a descending P stem. This kit contains a contour traced directly from the approved white-on-orange image, preserving the loop and stem silhouette. No previous hand-drawn logo geometry is used. The vector contour follows the source to within approximately one source pixel.

## Choose the right file
SVG: websites, Figma, Illustrator, scalable design. PDF and EPS: vector artwork for print vendors. PNG: slides, social platforms and general use. Width in filenames is the actual pixel width; height follows the artwork ratio. Transparent means genuine alpha transparency, not a checkerboard background. White and white transparent artwork can appear blank on white; inspect it against a dark background.

## Logo system
Use horizontal for website headers, stacked for centered layouts, symbol for icons, and wordmark when the symbol already appears nearby. Do not redraw, rotate, stretch, add effects or separate the loop and stem. Use the supplied geometry consistently. Keep at least 40 master units of empty space around the visible symbol (approximately the stem width). For lockups, keep at least one capital-O counter of clear space.

## Minimum sizes
Recommended symbol: 24 px digital / 8 mm print. At 16 px use the supplied full-bleed favicon; it is a convenience export, and 24 px or above preserves more detail. Horizontal lockup: 150 px / 40 mm. Stacked lockup: 120 px / 32 mm. Do not use the wordmark at favicon size. Inspect an actual-size proof for each print process.

## Color
Primary orange #FF5A00, white #FFFFFF, charcoal #151515. Black #000000 and white #FFFFFF are utility colors. Orange and white are the signature identity pairing. Use charcoal text for readable body copy on orange or white. Cream/white on orange is intended for brand artwork and large display elements; do not assume it meets small-text accessibility contrast requirements. Use white on charcoal for small reversed text. RGB/HEX values are authoritative for digital. CMYK values in palette.json are mathematical approximations, not press-profile conversions. This kit does not specify a Pantone match. Obtain a color-managed proof before production.

## Typography
Wordmark: Arial Bold converted to editable vector outlines, optically placed beside the symbol. Body typography: Arial Regular; headings: Arial Bold. Suggested web stack: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif. Font binaries are not redistributed. Use a properly licensed system installation or replace body typography with your licensed company font. The supplied outlined wordmark does not require a font installation.

## Backgrounds
Preferred: white mark on orange; orange mark on white; white on charcoal; black on white. Transparent files may be placed on a quiet contrasting background. Avoid busy photography, similar-value backgrounds and unapproved gradients. Square icon exports are full bleed: platforms apply their own corner masks. Do not add an extra inset square.

## Social and web
Includes avatar, square post, story, X header, LinkedIn company cover, YouTube banner and Open Graph image. Keep essential YouTube content in the central safe area and preview across devices. Platform crop rules may differ. Web files include a multi-resolution favicon.ico, apple-touch-icon, 192/512 icons and a starter manifest. Change start_url for your app and serve files from /brand/ or update paths in the snippet. A dedicated mobile OS adaptive/monochrome icon package is not included.

## Templates
Business card artboards are 1050 x 600, equivalent to 3.5 x 2 inches at 300 ppi, with no bleed. Ask your printer to add the required bleed, trim marks and color profile. Letterhead is A4 proportional. The slide cover is 16:9. Templates contain explicit placeholder text: replace your name, role, email, website and company details. No company address, domain or legal status has been invented. These are editable starting templates, not completed legal or print stationery.

## Brand voice
Clear, direct and helpful. Explain what a peer can do next. Prefer concrete wording over technical claims. Do not claim guaranteed returns, security certifications or regulated status without evidence. No tagline is prescribed.

## Handover
All SVG logo masters contain path geometry, no external images, dependencies or live logo text. Print PDFs retain vector paths; EPS uses RGB colors. The brand-guide PDF is a high-resolution visual document. Source generator is included for reproducibility. This is a starter identity system; trademark clearance, custom font licensing and final press preparation remain separate production tasks.
