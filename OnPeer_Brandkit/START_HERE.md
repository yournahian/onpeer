# OnPeer professional brand kit

Start with 08_Guidelines/OnPeer_Brand_Guidelines.pdf or BRAND_GUIDELINES.md.

- 01_Vector_SVG: outlined symbol, horizontal, stacked and wordmark masters.
- 02_PNG: transparent and background variants, up to 4096 px.
- 03_Print_Vector: vector PDF and RGB EPS counterparts.
- 04_App_and_Web: square app icons, favicon, Apple icon, manifest, HTML snippet.
- 05_Social: profile, post, story and cover artwork.
- 06_Templates: editable card, letterhead, slide cover and email signature.
- 07_Color_and_Tokens: palette JSON, CSS and GPL swatches.
- 08_Guidelines: brand guide and preview board.
- 09_Source: master construction data and reusable export generator.

Color filenames say exactly what they contain. Transparent white logos should be viewed on a dark canvas. Pixel sizes are image widths. No font binaries are bundled. Templates have placeholders to replace. Print exports need printer-specific profiling/bleed preparation.
